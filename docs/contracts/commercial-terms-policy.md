# Commercial Terms and Access Policy

## Policy

StoneCharts does not promise a commercial license, contribution agreement, support
commitment, or external access model until those terms are explicitly approved and
recorded.

Until then, the project remains proprietary by default. External access, source
contributions, distribution rights, support expectations, and commercial use terms
must not be inferred from technical progress, repository visibility or access, or
internal planning status.

## Required approvals

Before any public commercial or contribution model is stated, the record must name:

- the license or access grant
- the contribution terms and IP assignment or license-back rules
- the support boundary and response ownership
- the distribution channels that are authorized
- the commercial use scope, if any

## Non-claim

This policy does not define the final commercial model. It states that no such model is
approved until a written business policy is adopted and tracked.
