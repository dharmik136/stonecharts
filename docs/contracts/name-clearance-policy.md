# Name Clearance Policy

## Policy

StoneCharts is not cleared for public commercial use until the dated clearance record
is completed and reviewed. The required record must include repository, package-index,
domain, company-name, and trademark searches, plus explicit jurisdiction and legal
review boundaries.

Technical adoption of the StoneCharts namespace does not constitute legal clearance.
Branding, publication, and public commercial statements remain blocked until the record
exists.

## Required record contents

- Search scope and date
- Search result summary
- Jurisdictions reviewed
- Legal-review boundary statement
- Proceed / constrain / rename decision

## Non-claim

This policy does not claim external clearance. It defines the evidence required before
the product may say the name is cleared.
