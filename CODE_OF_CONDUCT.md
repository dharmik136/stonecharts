# Code of Conduct

StoneCharts collaborators must communicate professionally, review technical claims on
evidence, protect confidential information, and separate criticism of work from
criticism of people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, exposure of private data,
or retaliation for good-faith defect and security reporting are not acceptable in
project spaces.

Concerns should be reported privately to the product owner or security contact as
appropriate. The project owner may remove access, reject contributions, or take other
proportionate action. Because the current project has one owner and no independent
conduct committee, this limitation is stated directly rather than implying an appeals
structure that does not exist.
